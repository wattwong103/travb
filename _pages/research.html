Research

How movement becomes evidence

TBRG works from travel decisions outward: surveys and models of how people move, then engineering and planning that can act on that evidence. The group is based in the Department of Civil Engineering at Chulalongkorn University.

  • Transportation engineering Safe, efficient movement of people and goods, and the systems that carry that movement.
  • Travel behavior surveys and analysis Mode, time, purpose, and the preferences behind those choices.
  • Transportation planning What the network needs next, from current use to future demand.

Published work from the group also includes mass-transit ridership and land use in Bangkok, electric-vehicle adoption, and car-sharing scenarios. Full citations live on Publications and Google Scholar.
