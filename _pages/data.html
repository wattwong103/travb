Open data

We share research data when privacy and project agreements allow.

Walk access to urban rail

Isochrones, station exits, khet boundaries, and a 16:9 poster for the Access map. Built from OpenStreetMap (ODbL). Speed 4.5 km/h on the walk graph; 10- and 15-minute unions from station exits.

  • classes.geojson — white <10 min, orange 10–15, red >15
  • stations.geojson
  • khet.geojson
  • rail.geojson
  • station_iso.geojson — per-station 10/15 min polygons
  • access-16x9.png — 16:9 poster
  • access-16x9.jpg — lighter still of the same map
  • meta.json — speed, date, station count

Cite as Travel Behavior Research Group, Chulalongkorn University, walk-access isochrones to urban rail (Bangkok), plus OpenStreetMap contributors.

How we treat data

  • Privacy Personal and sensitive fields are removed or anonymized before any share.
  • Documentation Shared files come with enough context to reuse them.
  • Formats Machine-readable where possible.
  • Citation Use the paper or the dataset citation we send with the files.
