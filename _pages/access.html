How it is built

  • Origins Station exits (OSM subway entrances), not the station centroid. If no exit is mapped, the station node is used.
  • Network OpenStreetMap walk graph. Speed 4.5 km/h (75 m/min). 10 minutes = 750 m on the network; 15 minutes = 1,125 m.
  • Unions Isochrone per exit, unioned by station, then across all stations. White is the 10-minute union; orange is 15 minus 10; red is the study area minus 15.
  • Study area All 50 Bangkok districts, plus districts in Nonthaburi, Samut Prakan, and Pathum Thani that contain a selected station.
  • Lines BTS Sukhumvit, Silom, and Gold; MRT Blue, Purple, Yellow, and Pink; Airport Rail Link; SRT Dark Red and Light Red. Not BRT, boats, conventional SRT, or unopened Orange.

Parks, rivers, and expressway boxes stay red when they have no walkable OSM edges. Missing exits in OSM make some stations look worse than they are. Speed is a pipeline setting, not a live control — the pages site cannot recompute the graph.

Related lab work

The Public Transport Accessibility Index (PTAI) is a demand-weighted generalized-cost index for 88 inner-Bangkok subdistricts. This map is only the walk to urban rail. They answer different questions; PTAI routing data cannot be republished here.
