# How movement becomes evidence

The Travel Behavior Research Group integrates transportation engineering, behavioral psychology, data science, and urban planning to understand complex mobility patterns and develop evidence-based solutions that enhance transportation systems, promote sustainable travel choices, and improve accessibility for diverse populations across multiple spatial and temporal scales.

<ul class="theme-list">
  <li>
    <strong>Mobility pattern analysis</strong>
    Understanding how people move through urban and rural environments using GPS tracking, mobile data, and machine learning to identify travel behaviors and preferences.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Transportation choice modeling</strong>
    Developing predictive models that explain why people choose specific modes of transport, routes, and travel times based on personal, social, and environmental factors.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Smart mobility systems</strong>
    Integrating behavioral insights with emerging transportation technologies like autonomous vehicles, shared mobility, and multimodal planning platforms to enhance user experience and system efficiency.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Travel demand forecasting</strong>
    Creating advanced models to predict future transportation needs using demographic trends, land use patterns, and behavioral change indicators for infrastructure planning.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Sustainable transportation behavior</strong>
    Investigating factors that influence adoption of eco-friendly travel modes and designing interventions to promote low-carbon mobility choices.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Transportation equity and accessibility</strong>
    Analyzing how transportation systems serve different socioeconomic groups and developing strategies to improve mobility access for underserved communities.
  </li>
</ul>
